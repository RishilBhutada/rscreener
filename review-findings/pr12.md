## Checked and not reported

- `crore()` in the results alerts divides by 1e7. `results_history` stores plain rupees (`fetch_results_history.py` scales lakh and crore figures up to rupees), so the ₹ Cr figures are right.
- `account_list.py` reads the Firestore profile in the shape the app writes it (`sync.ts` and `watchlists.ts`: `{lists:[{symbols}]}` and holdings with a numeric `qty`).
- The gold export's `"surprise" in fam`: `fam` is the family's dict there, not its name, so that check works.
- `export_json.py --symbols` (patch mode): the per-company inputs are limited to the chosen companies through temporary tables. I found no figure that is ranked or averaged across all companies and would come out wrong for the patched rows.

Not verified (no network here): the exact field values NSE returns for SAST `promoterType` and for bulk/block deal quantities. If they differ from what `fetch_news.py` assumes, the "(promoter)" tag or the whole deals step could be wrong or fail.
